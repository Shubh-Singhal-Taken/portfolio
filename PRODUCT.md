# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening Shubh Singhal for one of three roles: Software Engineer, AI Engineer, or IoT Engineer. Most arrive from a link in the matching resume, often on a laptop between other candidates, and decide within the first screen or two whether to keep reading. A smaller group are collaborators, hackathon organisers and students from the CSED community who want to see his work.

The job to be done: confirm, quickly and with evidence, that he is strong in the role they are hiring for, then reach him or download the resume for that role.

## Product Purpose

One portfolio presented through three lenses. The same content (projects, experience, recognition) is selected and ordered differently for Software, AI and IoT, each at its own URL so every resume can link to the page made for it. A neutral front page introduces him across all three and opens the door to each lens.

Success: a recruiter who lands on a lens page finds the two or three most relevant projects, the matching experience and the right resume without hunting, and leaves remembering the site.

## Brand Personality

Bold, inventive, cinematic. The site is itself a piece of work: a WebGL star field, a star-portrait that forms on load and unwinds into a turning galaxy, real motion with a reason behind it. The voice is confident and specific: concrete numbers and named systems ("50 REST APIs", "LSTM on Azure", "Pixhawk"), never adjectives standing in for evidence.

Emotional goal: "this person builds ambitious things and finishes them."

## Anti-references

- **Generic developer template.** Hero, three equal cards, skill bars, a vertical timeline: the portfolio every bootcamp produces. If a section could be swapped into any other portfolio unchanged, it is wrong.
- **Gimmick over substance.** Effects that delay or hide the content: forced loaders, custom cursors, sound that asks to be enabled, content revealed only on hover, scroll-jacking that fights the reader. Spectacle must never cost a recruiter time.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before adjectives.** Every claim on the page is backed by a project, a number or a named system next to it.
2. **Cinematic, never in the way.** Motion and 3D carry meaning (one person, three disciplines; a portrait becoming a galaxy) and always run beside the content, never in front of it.
3. **Right lens, right reader.** Each profile page leads with what that role's hiring manager is looking for; nothing irrelevant above the fold.
4. **One source of truth.** Content lives once and is composed per lens, so the three pages can never contradict each other or the resumes.
5. **Finished, not placeholder.** No "coming soon", no dead links, no fake success states. If something is not ready, it is not shown.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, full keyboard operation with visible focus, pinch zoom allowed, and every animation (particles, galaxy, 3D scene, reveals) collapses to a static or crossfaded state under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content never depends on hover alone, so touch users see everything.
